Chronicle of Pure Quality

Seven Decades of Doing Things the Right Way

While the fast-food world embraced pre-frozen patties and automated short-cuts, In-N-Out stayed obsessively analog.

The Genesis

1948: The First Drive-Thru Stand

In Baldwin Park, California, Harry Snyder invented the revolutionary two-way speaker box system, pioneering modern drive-thru dining. Every hamburger was cut, trimmed, and made strictly to order from day one.

'48
Baldwin Park, CA
Classic Neon Signage
'54
Visual Landmark

1954: The Iconic Arrow Appears

The famous neon yellow boomerang arrow was introduced, soaring above the roofline to guide hungry road-trippers to the friendliest service and freshest beef on the West Coast.

Culinary Legend

1961: The Animal Style® Tradition

Customers began asking for patties seared in pure mustard, extra spread, pickles, and sweet caramelized grilled onions. Thus, our first and most revered “Secret Menu” legend was born.

'61
Mustard-Cooked & Caramelized
The 'X' Marks the Spot
'75
Signature Touch

1975: Real Shakes & Crossed Palms

Harry Snyder planted crossed palm trees at every new restaurant, inspired by his favorite film “It's a Mad, Mad, Mad, Mad World”—signaling to travelers that real 100% ice cream shakes and treasure lay right inside.

Vertical Perfection

1992: Our Own Butchers & Bakeries

To safeguard uncompromising flavor, In-N-Out operates its own artisanal butchery facilities and bun bakeries. We deliver daily within a strict 300–400 mile radius—guaranteeing no patty is ever frozen or pre-packaged.

'92
100% Whole Chuck Beef
