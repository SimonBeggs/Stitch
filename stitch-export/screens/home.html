Our Heritage

Harry & Esther Snyder’s Promise

In 1948, Harry Snyder opened California’s first drive-thru hamburger stand in Baldwin Park. Every morning before dawn, Harry visited meat and produce markets to pick the freshest ingredients by hand, while Esther carefully handled the books from their home down the street.

Their philosophy remains untouched: “Give customers the freshest, highest quality foods you can buy and provide them with friendly service in a sparkling clean environment.”
