Ingredient Transparency & Allergens

We are committed to total honesty. We cook all fried items exclusively in 100% high-grade sunflower oil. Our sponge buns contain wheat, and our spread contains eggs. Gluten-free ordering is seamlessly supported with our Lettuce-Wrapped "Protein Style".
